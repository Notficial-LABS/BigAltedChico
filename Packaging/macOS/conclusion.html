BigAltedChico is ready to try.

The destination follows the scope selected in Destination Select:

All users: open the Standalone in /Applications (Applications in Finder's sidebar). Plug-ins are in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.

Only me: open the Standalone in ~/Applications, inside your home folder. Plug-ins are in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.

VST3 is in the VST3 subfolder; AU is in Components. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW after installing.

New instances start in Clean. Raise Mix to hear the processing. LIVE: 30 ms; Quality: 150 ms, plus host and audio-device buffering.

This is a local test build. Public release signing remains pending.
